# Ecoinza Project Memory

## Product Direction

Ecoinza should be positioned as a borderless financial infrastructure platform, not only a crypto landing page or simple payment service. The product should feel useful for businesses that need to receive, hold, convert, and move money across markets with fiat, stablecoin, merchant, and card workflows.

## Reference App

Primary reference: PhotonPay

- URL: https://www.photonpay.com/
- Why it matters: PhotonPay presents itself as a payment operating system with business-facing modules for global payments, cards, wallets, checkout, payout, and financial operations.
- Use it as a strategic reference for product framing, feature grouping, trust signals, and dashboard-style clarity.
- Do not clone the visual design directly. Borrow the structure and level of product confidence, then make Ecoinza feel distinct and relevant to African and global trade use cases.

## Positioning Notes

- Prefer "borderless financial infrastructure" or "payment operating system" language over generic crypto wording.
- Lead with practical business outcomes: accept payments, settle faster, manage wallets, convert currencies, pay suppliers, and reconcile transactions.
- Crypto and stablecoins should be framed as settlement and treasury tools, not speculative assets.
- Trust and compliance should be visible early: regulated operations, KYC/KYB, transaction records, secure custody, and reliable support.

## Expected Product Modules

- Business wallet
- Multi-currency balances
- Stablecoin savings or settlement
- Local and international transfers
- Merchant checkout
- Payment links and invoices
- Supplier and payroll payouts
- Virtual cards
- Currency conversion
- Transaction history and reconciliation
- Compliance, KYC, and KYB flows

## Target Users

- African businesses trading internationally
- Importers and exporters
- Online merchants and platforms
- Freelancers, agencies, and service businesses
- Teams paying suppliers, contractors, or staff across borders
- Users who want easier fiat-to-stablecoin and stablecoin-to-fiat movement

## Website And UI Direction

- Keep the experience product-led rather than purely promotional.
- Show actual workflows, balances, transactions, cards, payment links, and checkout screens where possible.
- Use concise benefit-led copy.
- Avoid making the site feel like only a crypto brand; it should also read as serious fintech infrastructure.
- Favor clear modules, strong hierarchy, direct calls to action, and trust markers.
- The landing page should quickly answer: what Ecoinza does, who it is for, what money rails it supports, and why it is safe to trust.

## Copy Cues

- Borderless payments for modern businesses
- Accept any currency. Settle in the one you use.
- Move money between fiat and stablecoins with confidence.
- One wallet for collections, conversions, payouts, and records.
- Built for African businesses operating globally.
